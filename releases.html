What's Changed

  • fix(datastore): a serve-mode checkout never keeps a dropped unit's save over a commit it sent, in a catch-up or a recovery a root waits on (swamp-club#4257) (#3533)

Summary

Fixes swamp-club#4257, a follow-up to swamp-club#4249 (#3530) raised by that PR's adversarial review. In a serve-mode checkout (format 3, serverAddress set), #3530's own-work skip (holdsOwnWork) applied whenever any unit in the process was open, and in every replay. Two edges let it keep content that never committed.

Edge 1: a root waiting on recovery.

  • runRoot counts itself as open before it waits on units.recovering.
  • A root that arrived while recoverDroppedWork was replaying therefore made the replay skip the dropped unit's checkpoint.
  • The dropped save stayed in the file, and the sweep of unowned files left it there.

Edge 2: another root open.

  • Unit A sent checkpoint X, then dropped its later save while root B was open.
  • A catch-up during B applied X to state and catalog but left A's dropped save in the file.
  • Until teardown, B read content that never committed.

The fix.

  • A dropped unit forgets the commits it sent.
  • Each unit notes the txnId and partition of every commit before handing it to the sink, so an append that fails but lands anyway is still counted.
  • When the unit drops, UnitTracker.dropped reports those txnIds and the v3 handle removes them from units.sent. A catch-up then puts the checkpoint back over the dropped save.

What's Changed

  • fix(serve): a takeover holds back only writes to a key whose kept lease is not adopted yet, an accept keeps the content until its change replicates, and deposits are bounded before they are parsed (swamp-club#4258) (#3532)

Summary

Follow-ups to swamp-club#4195 in serve's datastore API (swamp-club#4258).

1. A takeover no longer stalls every write. While a new leader adopts kept leases, a commit, lease request, forced release, renewal, release or quarantine accept waits (503 serve_not_leader) only when its own key's kept lease is not adopted yet. While the kept leases cannot be listed, every write still waits. The lease listing waits for the whole adoption, since until then it would leave out the leases not adopted yet. A commit and an accept are checked again under the forwarded-commit lock. An adoption retry never holds back a lease granted while adopting.

2. An accept confirms replication before it marks the change accepted and drops the held content. Otherwise it answers a retryable 503 and the change stays held. An accept of a change that already landed (unconfirmed replication, or a stop before marking) is confirmed and marked without landing it again. That only happens when the landed commit is the author's; otherwise it is refused and stays held.

3. Deposits are bounded before parsing. A deposit takes one of its caller's upload slots (MAX_CONCURRENT_CHUNK_REQUESTS, shared with commits and chunk uploads) before its body is read.
